About This Project

Placement Tracker System is a lightweight web application designed to help colleges and universities manage and track student placement data easily and effectively.

With this system, administrators can:

  • Insert and maintain student placement records
  • View and search existing records quickly
  • Analyze placement statistics and company trends

Our goal is to simplify placement data management, improve transparency, and help institutions better support students in their career journeys.
